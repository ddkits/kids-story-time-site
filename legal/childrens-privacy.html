Kids & Families — Kids Story Time

Effective date: 29 August 2026 Last updated: 29 August 2026

A one-page summary for parents, guardians and teachers. Everything here is a description of how the app actually works. The full details are in our Privacy policy.

Kids Story Time is a 3D storybook app for children aged 3 and up, made by Reallexi LLC. Twenty stories — ten for bedtime, ten for the daytime — read aloud in a warm voice, with a living 3D scene or a plain paper-book page, whichever your child prefers.

What a parent needs to know

No ads. None. Not banners, not videos, not "offers", not cross-promotion for other apps. There is no advertising software inside the app at all.

No tracking. We do not measure what your child reads, how long they read, or what they tap. No analytics tool is installed. The app never touches your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone and iPad, Advertising ID on Android), and nothing follows your child to another app or website.

No accounts, no sign-in. The app never asks for a name, an email address, a birthday, a photo or a password. There is nothing to create and nothing to remember.

No data collection. We collect no personal information from your child, and none from you either. The single exception is a membership purchase: the store's receipt and a random install number go to our server so the stories unlock — no name, no email, nothing that identifies anyone. That is the only data the app ever sends anywhere.

Everything stays on the device. Reading progress, the chosen reading mode, the narration and sound switches and all accessibility settings are saved on the device only. They never reach us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

It works completely offline. All twenty stories are inside the app when it is downloaded. Aeroplane, car, cabin, a house with no Wi-Fi — the stories all work, including read-aloud.

No microphone, no camera, no location. The app never asks for them and never uses them. The read-aloud voice is the one already built into your device; nothing is recorded and nothing is sent to a voice service.

No chat, no links out, no strangers. There is no messaging, no comments, no social features and no way for anyone to contact your child through this app. There are no links to outside websites inside the reading experience.

Purchases are behind a parental gate. Two stories are free forever. The rest are opened by the Story Time Membership ($3.99 per month or $42.99 per year). Before any purchase button appears, the app asks a multiplication question a young child cannot answer — and Apple or Google then asks for your password, Face ID or fingerprint. A child cannot buy anything on their own, and the app never pressures a child to ask you to.

Made to the children's rules. We follow COPPA in the United States and the GDPR (including children's data rules and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code) in the UK and EU — by the simplest route available: collecting nothing, profiling nobody, and showing no advertising.

Built for every child — all nine accessibility features

Kids Story Time ships every one of the nine accessibility features Apple asks apps to declare, and they are all on the Settings screen:

  1. VoiceOver and TalkBack — every control is properly labelled and announced, so a child or parent using a screen reader can navigate the whole app.
  2. Voice Control — every button can be spoken by the name it shows, so "Tap Settings" works.
  3. Larger Text — text scales from 100% up to 240%, and the app follows your device's text-size setting to start with. Pinch to zoom is never disabled.
  4. Dark Interface — a full dark theme (the default) and a light one, following your device setting until you choose.
  5. Differentiate Without Colour Alone — every state is shown with a word or a shape as well as a colour, for colour-blind readers.
  6. Sufficient Contrast — colours are checked against the WCAG contrast standard, with an extra high-contrast palette that follows your device's "Increase Contrast" setting.
  7. Reduced Motion — turns off the scene's gentle movement, camera drift and interface animations. It follows your device setting automatically.
  8. Captions — on-screen caption lines for the app's sounds.
  9. Audio Descriptions — spoken descriptions of what is happening in the picture, for children who cannot see the scene.

Narration, music and ambient sound each have their own switch, so a child who finds sound overwhelming can read in silence.
