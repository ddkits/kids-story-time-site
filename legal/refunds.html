Cancellation and Refunds — Kids Story Time

Effective date: 1 September 2026 Last updated: 1 September 2026

This page explains how to cancel a Story Time Membership in Kids Story Time, made by Reallexi LLC, and what happens to money already charged. It is the policy linked from our App Store and Google Play listings, and it lives at a fixed address so those links keep working.

The short version

You can cancel any time, and cancelling stops the next payment. We do not issue refunds.

We are not the shop. Apple and Google take the payment, hold the money, and are the only ones who can give any of it back. We never see your card and cannot put anything on it. If you want a charge that has already been taken returned to you, that is Apple's or Google's decision, made under their own rules — not ours.

Cancelling is the thing you probably want

Two different things, often confused:

  • Cancelling stops the next charge. Nothing is refunded, and nothing is cut off early: your membership carries on until the end of the period you have already paid for, and then it simply does not renew.
  • A refund is money already charged being given back. We do not do that. See below.

So if what you want is "stop taking my money", cancel — that is the whole answer, it takes under a minute, and you keep every story until the period you paid for runs out.

On iPhone or iPad

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kids Story Time → Cancel Subscription.

On Android

Open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kids Story Time → Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends: both stores charge the renewal a day ahead, and a cancellation made after that charge applies to the following period.

Refunds

We do not give refunds for a payment that has already been taken. A membership that has been charged for a month or a year is charged for that month or year; what cancelling changes is whether it renews again.

You may still ask Apple or Google directly. They are the merchant, they set the rules, and the decision is entirely theirs — we are not asked and we have no vote:

  • App Store: https://reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple ID that bought it. Governed by the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions.
  • Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory → Report a problem. Governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Play refund policy.

If either of them does issue a refund, the membership it paid for ends. The other eighteen stories close the next time the app can reach the store — because the app is built to work offline, that is the next time it has a connection, not the instant the refund is made.

What we will always do

  • Never penalise a cancellation. No block, no restriction, no "you cannot subscribe again". You are welcome back whenever you like.
  • Never touch reading progress. Progress and settings live on your own device. Cancelling does not delete them, and neither do we.
  • Keep the free stories free. The Sleepy Little Star and The Tortoise and the Hare stay open for good, membership or no membership.
  • Fix it if you paid and got nothing. If a charge went through and the stories did not unlock, that is a fault, not a refund question. Try Restore purchases on the membership screen first — it fixes it in seconds. If it does not, write to us and we will sort it out.

Your legal rights

Nothing here removes rights you have under the consumer law where you live. Some countries give a statutory right to cancel or be refunded that goes beyond what a shop chooses to offer; where that applies to you, it still applies, and Apple and Google administer it as the sellers of record.

Writing to us

Email admin@reallexi.io if a charge went through and the app did not unlock, if you are not sure which store took the payment, or if something about a charge does not make sense.

Please do not write to ask us for a refund — we cannot give one, and we would only be pointing you at Apple or Google, which you can do faster yourself with the links above.

What to include. Kids Story Time has no accounts — we have never held your name or email, so we cannot look you up. To find a purchase we need either the order id from the receipt Apple or Google emailed you (Google Play's look like GPA.####-####-####-#####; Apple prints "Order ID" on the receipt), or which store you bought from, which plan, and roughly when.

We reply within a few days.
