3. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, and you cancel through the store — not through us. We have no way to cancel a store subscription on your behalf.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the very top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Story Time Membership.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

(You can also get there through the App Store app: tap your photo or initials in the top-right corner, then Subscriptions.)

On Android

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Kids Story Time.
  5. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the steps.

(On a computer: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.)

What cancelling does. It stops future renewals. It is not a refund, and it does not cut you off straight away: your membership keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for. If you cancel a yearly plan a month in, you still have eleven months of stories. After that, the eighteen membership stories close and the two free stories carry on as normal.

To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before it starts.

Nothing is deleted when a membership ends. Reading progress and settings live on the device and are not touched. If you subscribe again — or tap Restore purchases on a new device while your membership is still active — everything opens again.

There is not much to misuse here — there is no chat, no uploads, no other players and no way to contact anyone through the app. Still, please use it lawfully, do not attempt to break, overload or probe our server, and do not use the app in a way that harms someone else's use of it.

We may suspend access to our purchase-verification server if it is being attacked or abused. We will not "ban" anyone from reading, and we have no way to reach into your device.

6. Offline play and availability

The stories are inside the app, so reading works with no internet at all. Our server is only ever needed to confirm a purchase, so a membership continues to work offline for an extended period before the app asks to check in again.

We try to keep the server up, but we do not promise it will never be down. If it is briefly unavailable, reading is unaffected; only a brand-new purchase or a restore has to wait.
